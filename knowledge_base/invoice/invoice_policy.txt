INVOICE PROCESSING POLICY

Document Type: Invoice

Rule 1:
Every invoice must contain an invoice number.

Rule 2:
Every invoice must contain an invoice date.

Rule 3:
Every invoice must contain a customer or vendor name.

Rule 4:
The invoice total must be greater than zero.

Rule 5:
If critical invoice information is missing, the document must be sent for manual review.

Rule 6:
Invoices with a total amount greater than 100000 require manual approval.

Rule 7:
The system must not invent missing information. Only information extracted from the document should be used.

Decision Rules:

APPROVED:
All mandatory information is available and no manual approval rule is triggered.

MANUAL_REVIEW:
Critical information is missing or the invoice requires manual approval.

REJECTED:
The document contains invalid or inconsistent mandatory information.
